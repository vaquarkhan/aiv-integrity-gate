What AIV stops now

Coherent conflict blocks introduced on eligible added lines, plus unauthorized Git mode 160000 introductions supported by trusted metadata.

Disclosure remains metadata. Lower-confidence findings remain advisory. Protected structural rules are unsuppressible, and full-run skip is disabled.
